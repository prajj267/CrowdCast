# Bibliography (master reference list)

All documents cite by these numbers. IEEE style.

**Verification status**
- **V** — existence and key bibliographic details (authors/venue/year) confirmed against an online source while preparing this documentation (Nov 2026).
- **K** — from established reference knowledge of a widely cited work; details believed correct but **must be checked against the publisher page/PDF before submission**. Page numbers in particular should be confirmed.

Before final submission, a team member ticks every row in the checklist (§4) after opening the original source.

Entries [40], [43] and [46] are further reading (forecasting practice, cross-scene counting, LightGBM as an XGBoost alternative) and are not cited in the text. Web sources [W5]–[W9] are the pages behind the dataset, licence and survey statements marked **[V]** in [03](../DOCUMENTATION/03_LITERATURE_REVIEW.md) and [12](../DOCUMENTATION/12_DATASET_AND_DATA_COLLECTION.md).
